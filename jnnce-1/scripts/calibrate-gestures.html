🎯 Gesture Calibration

Collect landmark data for each pose at multiple distances

Instructions

  1. Allow camera access when prompted
  2. Select a distance (Near / Mid / Far) — hold your hand at that distance
  3. Click a pose card to select it (it will highlight)
  4. Hold the pose steady — press Space or click Capture to record a sample
  5. Collect 20+ samples per pose per distance (more = better)
  6. Repeat for all 5 poses × 3 distances = 15 combinations
  7. Click Export JSON to save the calibration data

Poses: Fist (all fingers curled) | Open Palm (all extended) | One Finger (index only) | Two Fingers (index+middle) | Three Fingers (index+middle+ring)
